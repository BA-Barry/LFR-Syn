# Research progression and evidence boundaries

| Stage | Retained output | What it establishes and what remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original LFR direction | Failure attribution, local interventions and feedback-learning hypothesis | The original aim was better computed synthesizability; software construction was not itself generator efficacy. |
| 2 October manuscript | Task-guided reaction-constrained replacement on fixed outputs; eight-run validation and controls | Concrete workflow evidence, including negative Mix results; no route-feedback training of those generators. |
| 6 October manuscript | Sixteen new optimizer runs, five policies, fixed task floors, score/diversity reporting | Large qualification-policy gains; B883/C880 does not show universal algorithm superiority. These changed rules are an explicit part of the result. |
| Through 18 October | Existing 175-node evolution record, including direct edit/route tests, failures and stopped prototypes | Several useful local effects and assets, plus negative mechanism/transfer results. A successful local test does not prove the original feedback-learning chain. |
| 21–29 October | Structural problem decomposition, cross-field analogies and contribution interpretation | Research redesign and evidence review, not another efficacy cohort. Similar prior work motivates concrete comparisons and added value rather than automatic rejection. |
| 4 November | FICEL six-algorithm scheme and theoretical revisions | Typed evidence, root outcomes, source effects, lesson use and actual learning/evaluation interfaces; no complete six-module execution. |
| 6–7 November | V5.2.4–V5.2.5 finite instance and computation/interface supplements | One-batch update semantics, bounded hierarchical calculations, identity/replay/cost scope and 19 handcrafted numerical checks; new molecular efficacy remains untested. |
| 19 November | This public repository | Deposit and organization of existing writing, source, saved data and evolution. No new molecular/model experiment. |

The original 175 tree records are retained; four clearly marked design/review nodes extend the public tree to the latest local research revision. Record counts are not counts of independent experiments. Historical “current” decisions and “private” labels are dated, and the current research is identified explicitly rather than overwriting scientific history.

Some evidence remains indexed but is not distributed here. The tree labels those references as historical indexes; it does not create broken machine-local links or imply that every original graph, model or record has been released. Complete per-molecule reruns need external assets described in the archived package.
