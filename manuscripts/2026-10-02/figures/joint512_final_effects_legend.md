# 新512固定生成槽位的最终联合端点图

**Figure. Fixed-slot joint yield and paired policy effects in eight complete terminal runs.** (a) Outputs meeting the optimized RF task cutoff and a saved, audited Graph2Edits route criterion. Task cutoffs are 0.10 for GSK3B and 0.05 for JNK3. (b) The same fixed outputs evaluated with the published ten-model-per-task Chemprop ensemble at a mean prediction cutoff of 0.5, together with the unchanged route criterion. Counts and percentages retain all 512 original generation slots, including 21 invalid sources. A denotes Morgan-ranked reaction-compatible precursors; B uses task-specific RF path contributions; C uses permuted contributions; Mix alternates B and A proposal streams before task-score selection. The route trigger intervenes on valid route-unresolved sources; the joint trigger additionally includes sources with an existing audited route but an insufficient RF task score. The first-candidate policy allows up to 12 candidate planner calls; sequential 6+6 policies evaluate at most two candidates within the same total call allowance and shared 45-second soft search allowance. Original sources remain the fallback. SynTwins bars show the known lower bound and a pale extension for eight execution-limited source decisions, giving RF-and-route yield bounds of 153–161 and Chemprop-and-route bounds of 58–66. These scientific unknowns are not unfinished jobs. (c) Paired policy differences in joint yield, in percentage points of all raw slots. Blue circles show the optimized RF endpoint; orange squares show the Chemprop sensitivity. Lines are conditional 95% empirical percentile intervals from resampling the four paired complete training-run effects within each of the two fixed tasks, enumerating all 65,536 combinations. The recorded primary contrast is Mix minus B under joint triggering and 6+6 allocation; the other shown contrasts are recorded secondary comparisons. These intervals describe run variation under the fixed tasks, without assuming slot independence or applying a multiplicity adjustment. The primary Mix comparison did not improve yield. Prediction scores and computational route audits do not establish experimental activity or laboratory synthesis.
